{{CARDS}}

Observations

  • The product shots are usable. With the same seed the composition stays consistent, and the lining colour and the flower bartack at the pocket corners show in all four. However, the flowers are drawn large, like embroidery, rather than as a thread bartack (1–1.5cm).
  • Only Ink navy gained a drawstring. Even with the same seed, details change when the colour changes. A comparison board has to state details explicitly, for example "no drawstring".
  • Apricot came out orange. It skews towards higher saturation.
  • The on-model shots ignore some instructions. They ignored "hood down" and all four show the hood up, added a drawstring that was not in the product shots, and lost most of the flower bartacks. Small signature details drop out in the on-model composite.
  • Colours generally come out lighter. Only Ink navy is close to the target (ΔE 3.6). Chestnut, Deep olive and Charcoal are noticeably off at 8.5–10.9.

How to read this

  • ΔE00 is the difference between the target outer fabric colour and the colour at the centre of the rendered body panel. 1–2 is hard to tell apart by eye, 5 or more is immediately visible, and 10 or more reads as a different colour.
  • Renders are for checking direction only. Final colours are confirmed with swatches and a spectrophotometer.
  • The measurement is the median of a fixed patch at the centre of the body panel, so it is a rough value that mixes in lighting and shadow.
